Neural Simulation-Based Inference for EveNet

Sept 2025 – present

Integrating an NSBI head into EveNet, a foundation model for particle-collision data, to enable likelihood-free parameter estimation in high-energy physics. Undergraduate research at UW, advised by Prof. Shih-Chieh Hsu.

Gradient Descent Emerges via Natural Selection

July 2024 – Aug 2025

A genetic algorithm testing whether evolutionary processes can produce gradient approximators sufficient for neural-network learning — evolved update rules that recover gradient-descent-like behavior without backpropagation. Sole-author paper at IEEE CEC 2025.

Space-Time Curvature-Based Clustering

Sept 2023 – June 2024

Clustering reformulated with principles from general relativity, introducing the Curvature Density score — outperforming k-means and hierarchical baselines. First-author paper at IEEE AMLDS 2025.
